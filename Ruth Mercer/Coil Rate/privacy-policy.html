Privacy Policy for Coil Rate

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Coil Rate is a free iOS app from Ruth Mercer. It keeps each spring’s wire, mean diameter, coils, and rate on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Ruth Mercer at ruth.mercer66@163.com. The contact address is United States.
